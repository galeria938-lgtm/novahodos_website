## Executive Verdict

NOVAHODOS Website is strategically strong and no longer reads like a simple landing page. It communicates a real company architecture: NOVAHODOS as the adaptive-systems company and AMI as the first product. The strongest current idea is the commercial/product cycle: Navigation -> Precision Calibration -> Navigation again. That idea is differentiated, honest, and more valuable than a generic Basic/Premium pricing ladder.

The web is now in a coherence phase, not an invention phase. The next iteration should not add many new sections. It should reduce friction, sharpen CTA hierarchy, localize small remaining English labels in Spanish, and make the path from curiosity to Founding Program feel inevitable.

## 1. Fortalezas

1. NOVAHODOS and AMI are correctly separated.

NOVAHODOS feels like the company. AMI feels like the first product. This avoids trapping the company inside one app name and leaves room for future products.

2. The product story is differentiated.

The best strategic sentence is not a single headline; it is the cycle:

`Navigation -> Precision Calibration -> Navigation again`

This turns pricing into a product rhythm instead of a hierarchy. It protects the $19 plan and makes the $49 plan feel like a precision moment, not a permanent upsell.

3. AMI is positioned beyond fasting.

The website now communicates that AMI is about adaptive guidance, decision support, route, calibration, and continuity. That is much stronger than competing against timer apps.

4. Founding Program improved materially.

The page now says Founding Member, first community, first generation, shared learning, and early access. It no longer sounds like beta tester recruitment.

5. The Experience page adds proof.

`/ami/experience` is strategically important because it shows product behavior instead of only describing it. It should remain one of the main conversion pages.

6. The legal boundary exists without dominating the product.

This is the right balance. The site should not lead with fear, but it should keep clear boundaries available.

7. Two-language architecture is present.

The Spanish and English pages exist, and core pages are not simply English-only. This is a major commercial advantage.

## 2. Debilidades

1. Home is becoming dense.

The home page contains many strong ideas: daily change, recalculation, science, optimization, the 14h example, Navigation/Calibration, daily habit, and first week. Each idea is valid, but together they risk cognitive load.

2. CTA hierarchy is not fully settled.

Current CTA language includes:

- Experience your first week
- See AMI in action
- Explore the science
- Read the story
- How AMI works
- Apply for Early Access
- Start with AMI

These are good individually, but the site needs one primary conversion path.

3. Spanish footer labels remain partially English.

The global footer uses `Terms`, `Privacy`, `Use Boundaries`, `Data Consent`, and `Contact` even on Spanish pages. This is small technically but visible strategically.

4. Contact is still administrative.

The Contact page lists emails well, but it does not yet feel connected to the Founding Member pathway. For now that is acceptable, but it should not become the main conversion destination.

5. Science supports credibility but does not yet feel like a narrative.

The science page is useful, but it still reads as a set of topics. It could be stronger as a simple sequence: why metabolism changes, why static rules fail, why signals matter, why calibration improves direction.

## 3. Riesgos

1. Over-explaining AMI.

AMI is complex. The web should make it simple. Adding every good idea risks making the visitor work too hard.

2. Diluting the core message.

The strongest core is:

`Your metabolism changes. AMI adapts your next step.`

Every page should support this. Anything else is secondary.

3. Medical/regulatory ambiguity.

The current engineering language is better than medical-device language. Still, terms like metabolic health, labs, biomarkers, and optimization must remain careful. Do not imply diagnosis, treatment, cure, or guaranteed clinical outcomes.

4. Founding Program may still lack a concrete next action.

The page is strong emotionally, but once the user wants to join, the mechanism should become more concrete: short form, email capture, access code, or clear application flow.

5. Product screenshots can create privacy risk if real data is exposed.

Screenshots are powerful, but they must be sanitized and public-safe. Do not publish raw personal labs, names, codes, account emails, or identifiable longitudinal records.

## 4. Oportunidades

1. Make the main path explicit.

Recommended narrative path:

`Home -> AMI -> Experience -> Founding Program`

Science, FAQ, Founder, Pricing, and Legal support that path.

2. Turn the first week into the conversion bridge.

The phrase `Experience your first week with AMI` is strong. It makes AMI feel like something lived, not merely bought.

3. Use Pricing as strategic education.

Pricing should not only show $19 and $49. It should teach the cycle:

`Navigate daily. Calibrate when it matters. Return sharper.`

4. Improve Spanish polish.

Spanish is a commercial asset. It should feel native, not translated. Footer labels, accents, and CTA consistency matter.

5. Add lightweight trust proof later.

Not fake urgency. Not inflated claims. But eventually:

- founding member count,
- waitlist count,
- founder note,
- real testimonial after consent,
- anonymized case trajectory.

## 5. Inconsistencias

1. CTA vocabulary varies too much.

Suggested hierarchy:

- Primary site CTA: `Experience your first week` / `Vive tu primera semana`
- Final conversion CTA: `Apply for Early Access` / `Solicitar acceso temprano`
- Secondary CTA: `See AMI in action` / `Ver AMI en acción`

2. Company/product language should remain disciplined.

Use this hierarchy:

- NOVAHODOS = company and mission.
- AMI = product.
- Navigation = daily mode.
- Precision Calibration = recalibration mode.
- Experience = proof of product.
- Founding Program = community/conversion.

3. Footer legal labels are not fully localized.

This should be fixed in the next polish pass.

4. `Contact` is not yet integrated with the Founding Program journey.

If users apply through contact, the page should know that. If Founding Program becomes the true application page, contact can stay institutional.

5. Science and Experience are both proof pages, but their roles should stay distinct.

- Science explains why AMI's logic makes sense.
- Experience shows what AMI feels like in use.

## 7. Cambios Recomendados

1. Create one CTA rule.

Use one main CTA in the header and floating button:

English: `Experience your first week`
Spanish: `Vive tu primera semana`

On Founding Program, use the final CTA:

English: `Apply for Early Access`
Spanish: `Solicitar acceso temprano`

2. Localize footer items.

Spanish footer should use:

- `Términos`
- `Privacidad`
- `Límites de uso`
- `Consentimiento de datos`
- `Contacto`

3. Add journey bridges.

At the bottom of each major page:

- Home -> AMI or Experience
- AMI -> Experience
- How It Works -> Founding Program
- Science -> Experience or FAQ
- Founder -> Founding Program
- Pricing -> Founding Program

4. Keep the pricing cycle central.

Do not hide the Navigation/Precision idea. It is one of the strongest business insights on the site.

5. Tighten Science into a story.

Recommended sequence:

`Metabolism changes -> static rules fail -> signals reveal direction -> labs sharpen calibration -> AMI adapts the route.`

6. Add a Founding Access form later.

Minimal fields only:

- name,
- email,
- country,
- primary goal,
- labs available yes/no,
- consent checkbox.

No payment yet.

## 8. Cambios NO Recomendados

1. Do not add more pages before tightening the existing flow.

The site already has enough pages for iteration 1.

2. Do not turn Science into a scientific paper.

Science must build trust, not overwhelm.

3. Do not remove legal boundaries entirely.

The site should communicate engineering and precision, but legal clarity protects the company.

4. Do not use fake urgency.

No countdowns, fake scarcity, or invented user counts.

5. Do not publish raw personal lab PDFs or identifiable records.

Use sanitized, anonymized, cropped, and consent-safe visuals only.

6. Do not connect Stripe, PayPal, or billing yet.

Payment infrastructure can be prepared later, but current strategic priority is credibility, waitlist, and early access.

7. Do not import or couple code from AMI Engine.

The website explains. The app demonstrates. Keep the projects separate.

## Page-by-Page Notes

### Home `/` and `/es`

Strong opening. The idea that metabolism changes daily is the correct entry point. The risk is density: too many high-value sections can compete with each other. Home should make one promise, prove it lightly, and move the visitor to Experience or Founding Program.

### AMI `/ami` and `/es/ami`

This is currently one of the strongest pages. It makes AMI feel like a product system, not a timer. Keep the Navigation/Calibration cycle here.

### How It Works `/ami/how-it-works` and `/es/ami/how-it-works`

Clear and useful. It should stay short. It is the page that prevents confusion.

### Science `/ami/science` and `/es/ami/science`

Good credibility layer. Next pass should make it more narrative and less topic-list. Keep language for humans.

### Founding Program `/founding-program` and `/es/founding-program`

Strongest recent improvement. It now feels like belonging. Next step is a concrete application mechanism.

### Founder `/founder` and `/es/founder`

Correct direction: the founder is part of the mission, not the whole story. `Why AMI Exists` is better than a resume page.

### FAQ `/faq` and `/es/faq`

Useful support page. It should answer objections before registration. Make sure it supports the main conversion path and does not become a dumping ground.

### Contact `/contact` and `/es/contact`

Institutional and useful. Should remain secondary unless a dedicated Founding Program form is not yet available.

### Legal `/legal` and `/es/legal`

Appropriately present. Disclaimers should stay here and in compact safety references, not dominate the marketing pages.

### Pricing `/pricing` and `/es/pricing`

Important page. It should be treated as education, not only cost. It should reinforce: Navigation most days, Precision when it matters.

### Experience `/ami/experience` and `/es/ami/experience`

Very important for trust. Product proof belongs here. Keep all screenshots public-safe and sanitized.

## Final Recommendation

Do not start another major idea layer yet. Iteration 2 should be a coherence pass:

1. CTA hierarchy.
2. Spanish polish.
3. Journey bridges.
4. Mobile visual QA.
5. Public-safe product proof.

The site is close enough to public use that the next gains will come from clarity and trust, not from adding more concepts.
